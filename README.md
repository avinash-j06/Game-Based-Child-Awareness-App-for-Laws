## 📌 About the Project

**Rights4U** is a game-based educational web application created to make children aware of their **legal rights and important laws** through interactive and engaging learning experiences.

Instead of presenting legal information in a complex or difficult format, the platform uses **gamification, quizzes, story-driven challenges, and scenario-based simulations** to make learning more enjoyable and understandable.

The goal is to encourage children to learn about their rights while interacting with a simple and user-friendly digital platform.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* 📚 Create awareness about children's legal rights.
* 🎮 Make learning about laws fun and interactive.
* 🧩 Use gamification to improve engagement.
* ❓ Test knowledge through quizzes and challenges.
* 🎭 Present real-life situations through scenario-based learning.
* 🧒 Provide an easy-to-use and child-friendly interface.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Gamified Learning

Learn about important rights and laws through interactive activities instead of traditional text-based learning.

### 📝 Interactive Quizzes

Children can test their understanding through engaging quiz questions.

### 📖 Story-Driven Challenges

Educational content is presented through stories and challenges to make concepts easier to understand.

### 🎭 Scenario-Based Learning

Users can explore different situations and learn how rights and laws apply in real-life scenarios.

### 🌟 Child-Friendly Interface

The application is designed with simple navigation and an easy-to-understand interface suitable for children.

## 🎓 Learning Approach

The project follows a **Learn → Play → Understand** approach:

1. **Learn** – Introduce children to important rights and laws.
2. **Play** – Use quizzes, challenges, and interactive activities.
3. **Understand** – Help users connect what they learn with real-life situations.
